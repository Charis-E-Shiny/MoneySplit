# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in the Paisa Vasool project is expected to help keep its community respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give clear, actionable feedback focused on ideas and code.
- Welcome different perspectives and experience levels.
- Protect other people's privacy. Do not post credentials, personal information, or financial data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue. Maintainers should review reports promptly, keep them as confidential as practical, and take proportionate action.
